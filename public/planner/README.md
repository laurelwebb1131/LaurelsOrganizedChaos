# Laurel's Organized Chaos Planner

A free, local-first gothic scrapbook life planner.

## Step 1: personalization foundation
- Planner Edit Mode and normal View Mode
- saved page personalization and layouts
- draggable stickers and Polaroids
- page themes/background settings
- v2 to v3 data hydration

## Step 2: page-design completion
- finished visual identity across Dashboard, Today, This Week, Calendar, School, Projects, Home & Family, Brain Dump, Goals & Ideas, Life Scrapbook, and Settings
- scrapbook chapter tabs and page-specific witchy flourishes
- richer dashboard collage styling
- stronger Today anchor/timer spread
- weekly focus cards and scrapbook kanban treatment
- themed monthly calendar spreads for all seasons, with special October, November, and December treatments
- academic desk treatment for School
- laboratory ticket treatment for Projects
- practical home-board treatment for Home & Family
- lined panic-drawer treatment for Brain Dump
- tarot/constellation treatment for Goals & Ideas
- upgraded Polaroid wall and draggable scrapbook board
- responsive mobile/tablet polish
- reduced-motion support and lazy-loaded scrapbook images

## Core planner
Dashboard, Today, This Week, Calendar, School, Projects, Home & Family, Brain Dump, Goals & Ideas, Life Scrapbook, and Settings.

## Storage
Planner data remains in browser localStorage. Export JSON backups from Settings before clearing browser storage or changing devices.

## Cost
No backend, database, paid API, or subscription is required.
