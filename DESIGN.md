# Hearthwise visual direction

Hearthwise is a life operating system for people who want structure without self-punishment. The Today surface is an **Operate** dashboard dressed as a candlelit command center: the interface should feel like a well-kept room in the evening, with enough warmth to invite return and enough contrast to keep the next action obvious.

## Visual system

- **Foundation:** dark plum `#211a2d` for the navigation shell, with a warm parchment `#f4f0e8` work surface.
- **Signals:** moss green communicates care and completion; amber marks attention, lunar detail, and the primary moment.
- **Type:** Fraunces gives headings a tactile, bookish voice; DM Sans keeps controls compact and readable.
- **Shape:** 9–15px rounded work surfaces, thin parchment borders, quiet shadows. No glossy glass or decorative gradients.
- **Signature:** Juniper, the familiar, translates dashboard state into a short compassionate nudge.

## Composition

The navigation remains a dark vertical “hearth” on desktop and collapses to a compact wordmark on mobile. The first viewport leads with the actual shape of the day: pulse, tasks, check-in, familiar guidance, rhythm, and realms. Interactions are deliberately small and reversible: tasks toggle, mood selection confirms with a toast, and room navigation gives honest prototype states.
